Science Dashboard

This application applies conservative pediatric-oriented acoustic safeguards designed to reduce unnecessary overnight auditory stimulation.

Baby Mode enforces restricted gain, aggressive low-pass filtering and finite sleep cycles to reduce prolonged environmental masking exposure.

Brownian noise and low-frequency rhythmic masking are used because lower spectral density is generally perceived as softer and less intrusive than brighter broadband spectra.

The application avoids unrestricted continuous overnight loops in pediatric mode and applies automatic fade-out routines after finite sleep windows.

Microphone-based wake detection is processed locally on-device only. No audio leaves the device and no cloud recording is performed.

Audio calibration is heuristic and relative only. Browser APIs cannot guarantee medically exact SPL values across arbitrary mobile hardware.
